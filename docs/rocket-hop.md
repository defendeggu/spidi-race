# Camper rocket hop

Both Weltenbummler camper variants support a short cold-gas-inspired hop.

- Player 1 / solo: press **F**.
- Player 2: press **Numpad 2** or **Delete**.
- Touch: tap **ROCKET** at the top of the screen.
- The 0.4-second burst lifts the camper, followed by a gravity-driven landing.
- Each launch includes one 360-degree barrel roll around the vehicle's longitudinal axis. The roll starts after 0.3 seconds and finishes after 1.4 seconds, before a normal landing. Wheels rotate with the body; the chase camera stays upright.
- Cooldown lasts five seconds from ignition. Release and press again after landing.
- No midair retrigger, sustained flight, or launch from water is allowed.
- The HUD shows thrust, landing, cooldown and readiness. Normal driving controls remain available, with reduced steering authority while airborne.

This is an arcade mechanic inspired by cold-gas thrusters, not a simulation of Tesla hardware. Existing track barriers and town collision rules remain active. Gamepad users can trigger the hop with the keyboard; there is no dedicated gamepad binding.
